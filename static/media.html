Official Initiative | October 2026

15 GIRLS. ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP.

They earned their place. Let's help them reach the world stage.

Skynex Global has launched an initiative to raise donations and partner support for 15 young dancers from Belgrade, Novi Sad, and Vršac to attend the IDO World Show Dance Championships and IDO World Performing Arts Improvisation Championships 2026, held from December 5 to 9 in Poland.

The initiative brings together companies, organizations, and individuals wishing to contribute to funding travel, accommodation, competition fees, equipment, and other needs of the delegation comprising 15 competitors and five coaches.

Initial initiative goal: 35,000 EUR, through financial donations and direct partner support.

We invite all interested parties to get involved and support the young talents of Serbia.

Read the official call and find out how you can donate.
